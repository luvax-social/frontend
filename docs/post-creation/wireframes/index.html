Post creation - wireframes

Desktop wireframes at 1440 by 900 for every new surface in this phase, and for the existing surfaces whose layout changes.

Colours, type, radii and shadows are the live tokens from src/index.css; icons are placeholder outlines standing in for LxIcon.

Gold-tinted callouts are annotations, not interface.

3. Drafts and scheduled posts

P2-C1-R2 P2-C1-R3 P2-C1-R5
  • A drawer from the composer, so a draft can be resumed on any device from the same place.
  • Drafts and scheduled posts share one limit of 50, and the count is stated in words.
  • A scheduled post that failed returns here as a draft with the reason in plain language; the same reason is in a notification.
  • Scheduled posts offer "publish now" and "move to drafts"; drafts offer "resume" and "delete" with an inline confirm.

15. Admin, places registry

P2-C4-R5 Rule 7
  • The hashtag registry pattern: search, a status filter, one row per place and the actions that apply to its status.
  • Every action asks for a reason, because each writes an audit row; "delete" says it hides the place everywhere while posts keep their text.
  • Reports about a place land in the normal report queue; banning or deleting from there closes the linked report.
  • "unban" also brings a deleted place back, so there is no separate restore.
